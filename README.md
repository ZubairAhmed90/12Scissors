# ✂️ 12Scissors - Barber SaaS Platform

**12Scissors** is a modern SaaS platform built for barbershops and salons to manage bookings, staff, services, customers, and daily operations in one powerful system.

This platform helps businesses automate scheduling, reduce missed appointments, manage discounts, and deliver a smooth customer booking experience.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📅 Booking & Appointment System
- Online booking system for customers
- Real-time time slot availability
- Appointment status tracking (Pending, Confirmed, Completed, Cancelled)
- Auto prevention of double booking
- Booking history for customers and shops

### ⏱ Scheduling & Availability Management
- Barber-wise availability settings
- Custom working hours and shifts
- Blocked days / holidays / off days
- Configurable time slots (15 min, 30 min, 1 hour, etc.)
- Manual slot lock/unlock option

### 💰 Discount & Promo System
- Promo codes support
- Percentage and fixed amount discounts
- Limited-time promotions
- Minimum booking value rules
- Auto apply discount on checkout

### 🧾 Service & Pricing Management
- Add/edit services (Haircut, Beard Trim, Facial, Packages, etc.)
- Set service duration per service
- Pricing control per service
- Service categories and packages

### 👥 Customer Management
- Customer profiles with contact details
- Customer booking history
- Notes and preferences management

---

## 🏢 Multi-Role Dashboard System

**12Scissors** includes multiple user roles with separate dashboards and permissions.

---

### 👑 Admin Dashboard (Super Admin)
Admin has complete control over the platform.

- Manage all vendors (barbershops)
- Approve or block vendor accounts
- Manage all managers and staff users
- Control global platform settings
- View platform analytics & revenue insights
- Manage discount campaigns & promo codes
- Monitor all bookings across the system

---

### 🧑‍💼 Manager Dashboard
Managers handle day-to-day shop operations.

- Manage staff (barbers) schedules and availability
- View and manage all bookings
- Assign bookings to barbers
- Manage services, pricing, and time duration
- Manage customers and booking history
- Create and apply discounts/promotions
- Track daily/weekly/monthly performance

---

### 🏪 Vendor Dashboard (Barbershop Owner)
Vendors manage their own shop/business.

- Create and manage barbershop profile
- Manage shop staff (barbers)
- Manage services and pricing
- Set business hours and availability
- View shop bookings and revenue
- Manage discount offers
- Customer management and booking records

---

### 💇 Barber / Staff Dashboard
Staff members manage their own appointments.

- View assigned bookings
- Manage personal availability
- Track completed appointments
- Customer notes & service preferences
